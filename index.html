03 · Method

Method, evidence and factor sources

Emissions (t CO₂e) = activity quantity × emission factor (kg CO₂e per unit) ÷ 1,000. Library factors are typical published values from the US EPA GHG Emission Factors Hub, UK DESNZ/DEFRA conversion factors, worldsteel and IAI averages, the Mexican national grid factor (SEMARNAT/CRE) and IPCC AR6 100-year GWPs for refrigerants. They are reasonable defaults for screening. For a verified inventory, replace them with factors from your own sources, supplier EPDs or a licensed LCI database such as ecoinvent; edited factors are outlined so reviewers can see them.

Scope defaults follow the GHG Protocol: fuels and refrigerant leaks at your own sites are Scope 1, purchased electricity is Scope 2 (location-based), and everything upstream or downstream is Scope 3. Emission categories group lines by source type (materials, fuels, electricity, refrigerants, transport, waste and water), so the same category can appear in more than one scope. Each line also records the months its activity took place; its emissions are spread evenly across those months to build the monthly profile in the Gantt chart, so enter separate lines for seasonal bills if you need month-level accuracy.

Evidence status: a line is missing until a file is attached, attached once a document is on file, and verified when a reviewer confirms the document supports the quantity entered. Coverage is weighted by emissions. Attached files are stored only in this browser on this device and are not uploaded anywhere, so keep the originals in your document system.
