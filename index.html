Life cycle assessment · ISO 14040

What is a life cycle assessment?

A life cycle assessment (LCA) measures the environmental impact of a product across its whole life: from extracting the raw materials, through manufacturing, transport and use, to what happens when it is thrown away or recycled. It adds up what goes in (materials, energy, water) and what comes out (emissions, waste) at every stage, and turns them into impact indicators.

The method is set by ISO 14040 and ISO 14044. When an LCA looks at climate change only, the result is a carbon footprint in kg of CO₂-equivalent (ISO 14067), which is what CO2Trace calculates.

Why it matters

See where emissions really are

For many manufactured goods, a large share of the footprint sits in purchased materials or in product use, outside the company’s own sites. An LCA makes it visible.

Don’t just move the problem

A lighter material can cut transport emissions but raise production emissions. Looking at the whole life shows whether a change is a real improvement.

Meet reporting rules and buyer requests

Mexico’s RENE, Scope 3 under the GHG Protocol and California’s SB 253, the EU carbon border tax (CBAM) and buyers asking for EPDs all need emissions figures you can trace back to a source.

Back up every claim

Claims such as “low-carbon” or “30% less CO₂” need a documented study behind them (ISO 14021, ISO 14067). Without one, they read as greenwashing.

      03 · Method

      How every tonne is calculated

      Every line goes through the same formula. Factors keep each gas separately, so switching the GWP method recalculates everything without re-entering data.

      Full methodology notes

      Emission categories group lines by source type (materials, fuels, electricity, refrigerants, transport, waste and water), so the same category can appear in more than one scope. Each line also records the months its activity took place; its emissions are spread evenly across those months to build the monthly profile in the Gantt chart, so enter separate lines for seasonal bills if you need month-level accuracy.

      Attached files are stored only in this browser on this device and are not uploaded anywhere, so keep the originals in your document system.
